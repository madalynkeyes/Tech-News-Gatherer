Tech News Tool

Welcome to the Tech News Tool! This application fetches the latest tech news articles specifically about AI from various RSS feeds, filters them based on keywords, and stores them in a database for easy access. Gemini AI takes the latest 10 articles and creates a simplified summary of the top technology advancements of AI. To generate a new summary of the latest articles, click the "Generate AI Summary" button. This application is updated every two hours but can also be updated by clicking "Fetch Newest Articles" button below. Articles older than 7 days are automatically removed from the database and can also be removed immediately by clicking the "Cleanup Database" button below.
